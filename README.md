RefurbTrack

A Mobile-Based Phone Refurbishment Cost and Profit Tracking System

RefurbTrack is a React Native app for small cellphone repair shops. It records each phone's buy-repair-resell cycle and automatically computes total investment and profit or loss per unit. Built for AJ Cellphone Repair Shop and Accessories, Tagum City.

Features
Job type toggle: Buy & Resell or Customer Repair
Staff login (Firebase Authentication)
Phone intake, evaluation, and diagnosis records
Parts and labor expense tracking with a live running total
Unit status tracking, from Acquired to Sold / Unsold / Not Worth Repairing
Sale recording with automatic profit/loss calculation
Search and full refurbishment history per phone
Profit Formula
Total Investment = Purchase Price + Σ(Parts Cost) + Labor Cost
Profit / Loss    = Revenue − Total Investment
